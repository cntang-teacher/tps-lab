聚合函數把多筆記錄的某欄「摘要」成單一數值:COUNT 計數、SUM 求和、AVG 平均、MAX/MIN 最大最小。加上 GROUP BY,就會先按某欄分組,再對每組分別計算 —— 例如各班的平均分。左表以顏色標示分組。 Aggregate functions summarise a column across many records into one value: COUNT, SUM, AVG, MAX/MIN. Add GROUP BY to first split rows into groups by a column, then compute per group — e.g. the average score of each class. Colours on the left mark the groups.
