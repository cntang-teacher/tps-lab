ER 實體關係圖Entity-Relationship Diagram

選修A · ER Model
關係基數Cardinality
實體Entity 關係Relationship 屬性Attribute
ER 模型在設計數據庫時,以圖描述現實:實體 (矩形) 是要儲存的事物,屬性 (橢圓) 是其特性 (下橫線為主鍵),關係 (菱形) 連接實體。基數 (cardinality) 說明一方可對應另一方多少個:1:1、1:M、M:N。M:N 關係在轉為表格時,需要一個連接表拆解。 The ER model designs a database by drawing reality: entities (rectangles) are things to store, attributes (ovals) are their properties (underlined = primary key), and relationships (diamonds) link entities. Cardinality says how many of one side relate to the other: 1:1, 1:M, M:N. An M:N relationship needs a link (junction) table when turned into tables.
