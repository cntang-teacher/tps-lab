正規化逐步拆解表格,消除重複與異常。1NF:每格只存單一(原子)值,消除重複組。2NF:在 1NF 上,消除部分依賴(非主鍵屬性須依賴整個複合主鍵)。3NF:在 2NF 上,消除傳遞依賴(非主鍵屬性不可依賴另一非主鍵屬性)。結果減少冗餘,避免更新/插入/刪除異常。底線=主鍵,斜體=外鍵。 Normalization splits tables step by step to remove redundancy and anomalies. 1NF: each cell holds one atomic value — remove repeating groups. 2NF: on top of 1NF, remove partial dependencies (non-key attributes must depend on the whole composite key). 3NF: on top of 2NF, remove transitive dependencies (a non-key attribute must not depend on another non-key attribute). The result cuts redundancy and avoids update/insert/delete anomalies. Underline=PK, italic=FK.
